[ Orbit ]

Scientific Name:

Orbit

Discovered by / at:

Cecelia Chen, Oct 16, 2026

Family:

Celestial Organisms — a mysterious group of creatures originating from the cosmos.

Habitat:

Orbit lives in a dreamlike space between the deep ocean and the universe, filled with floating bubbles and distant stars.

Appearance:

Orbit has a glowing, flower-like body with six petals. Young Orbits are larger and purple, gradually becoming smaller and shifting toward blue and cyan as they mature.

Key Characteristics:

  • Bioluminescent.
  • Shrinks as it matures.
  • Sensitive to external disturbances.
  • Trapped in a repeating life cycle.

What we know so far about Orbit? Orbit follows an unusual life cycle. A young Orbit emerges from its glowing nest, accompanied by an adult that circles around it. As the young creature gradually shrinks and matures, it begins to move independently. The two briefly share the same space before the older Orbit returns to the nest, and disappears. Eventually, the younger one follows. Strangely, this is never the end. Both creatures reappear and repeat the same sequence, as if they are trapped in a fragment of time. No one knows what keeps them in this endless cycle, or whether they are even aware of it.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Orbit is sensitive to the presence of humans. Moving your cursor close to the young Orbit causes it to retreat, while clicking on it triggers a burst of purple light and sends the adult into a moment of panic. These interactions may interrupt their movements, but the creatures will always return to their nest, and the cycle will begin again.
