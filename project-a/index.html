[ Orbit ]

Scientific Name:

Orbit

Discovered by / at:

Cecelia Chen, Oct 16, 2026

Family:

Celestial Organisms — a mysterious group of creatures originating from the cosmos.

Habitat:

Orbit lives in a dreamlike space somewhere between the deep ocean and the universe. Floating bubbles and distant stars coexist in this strange environment, where time appears to be trapped in an endless loop.

Appearance:

Orbit has a glowing, flower-like body with six petals. Young Orbits are larger and purple, gradually becoming smaller and shifting toward blue and cyan as they mature. Cyan adults move and rotate freely.

Key Characteristics:

  • Bioluminescent.
  • Becomes smaller as it matures.
  • Adults circle around their young.
  • Sensitive to external disturbances.
  • Repeats the same life cycle.

What we know so far about Orbit? Orbit is a mysterious cosmic creature discovered in a world where the boundaries between the ocean and outer space seem to disappear. Unlike most living organisms, Orbit grows smaller as it ages. At the beginning of its life, a young Orbit emerges from a glowing nest, accompanied by an adult that continuously circles around it. As the young Orbit matures, its body gradually changes from purple to cyan. Eventually, it becomes independent, and the two creatures drift freely through their surroundings. After a short period together, the older Orbit returns to the nest, rotates briefly, and disappears. The younger Orbit eventually follows the same path. However, both creatures are trapped in a repeating fragment of time. When the cycle ends, everything begins again. It remains unknown whether Orbit experiences each life as something new or is simply reliving the same moment forever.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Orbit is highly sensitive to external disturbances. To interact with it, humans can either move their cursor near the young Orbit or click on it.When the cursor approaches, the young Orbit instinctively moves away to avoid potential danger. When clicked, it releases a burst of glowing purple particles, while the adult Orbit reacts with sudden, panicked movements to protect its young. Human interaction can disturb their movements, but it cannot break the time loop and change their fate.
